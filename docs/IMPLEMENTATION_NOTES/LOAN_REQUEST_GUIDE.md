# Loan & Salary Advance Request Guide

## How to Request a Staff Loan or Salary Advance

### Method 1: Via Payroll Section (Main Access Point) ⭐

1. **Navigate to Payroll:**
   - Click on **"Payroll"** in the left sidebar
   - Or go to: http://192.168.10.101:5000/#/payroll

2. **Go to Loans Tab:**
   - Click on the **"Loans & advances"** tab
   - You'll see all existing loans and advances

3. **Click "Request Loan / Advance" Button:**
   - Look for the green button: **"+ Request Loan / Advance"**
   - Click it to open the loan request form

4. **Fill Out the Form:**
   - **Employee:** Select yourself (auto-selected for employees)
   - **Loan Type:** Choose from dropdown:
     - Salary Advance
     - Emergency Loan
     - Staff Welfare Loan
   - **Principal Amount (GHS):** Enter amount needed
   - **Monthly Installment (GHS):** Enter monthly payment amount
   - **Repayment Months:** Select number of months (1-36)
   - **Start Date:** When deductions should start
   - **Purpose / Reason:** Explain why you need the loan

5. **Review Auto-Calculation:**
   - The system shows real-time preview:
     - Total amount
     - Monthly deduction
     - Number of months
     - Projected completion date

6. **Submit Request:**
   - Click **"Submit Request"** button
   - You'll receive a reference number (e.g., LOAN-2026-0001)
   - Request sent for approval

---

### Method 2: Via Your Profile (Quick Access)

1. **Go to Your Profile:**
   - Click your name/avatar in top right
   - Select **"My Profile"**
   - Or go to: http://192.168.10.101:5000/#/profile

2. **Navigate to Compensation Tab:**
   - Click on the **"Compensation"** tab
   - Scroll to **"Staff Loans & Advances"** section

3. **Click "+ Request Loan":**
   - Small button in the loans section
   - Opens the same loan request form
   - Follow steps 4-6 from Method 1 above

### Workflow:
```
Employee Request → HOD Review → Accountant Review → MD Approval → Active
```

### Status Indicators:
- 🟡 **Pending** - Awaiting approval
- 🟢 **Active** - Approved and deductions active
- 🔴 **Rejected** - Request declined

## Repayment Details

### Automatic Payroll Deduction:
- Deductions start on the **Start Date** you specified
- Amount is deducted **monthly** from your salary
- Deductions appear on your **payslip** each month
- Continue until loan is **fully repaid**

### Early Repayment:
- Contact HR/Payroll to make extra payments
- Can pay off loan early with no penalty
- Outstanding balance updated in real-time

### If You Leave the Company:
- Outstanding balance deducted from **final settlement**
- Includes: Last salary, leave pay, terminal benefits
- Automatically handled by system

## For Managers/HR/Payroll Staff

### Approve Loan Requests:
1. Go to **Payroll** → **Loans & advances** tab
2. Find pending loans (🟡 Pending status)
3. Click **"Track"** to review details
4. Click **"Approve"** or **"Reject"** button
5. Approved loans become active

### Manual Repayment Recording:
1. Click **"Track"** on any active loan
2. Scroll to **"Manual Repayment"** section
3. Enter:
   - Amount paid
   - Payment date
   - Source (Payroll/Cash/Bank)
   - Notes
4. Click **"Record Repayment"**
5. Balance updates automatically

## Common Questions

**Q: How long does approval take?**  
A: Typically 1-3 business days, depending on approver availability.

**Q: Can I have multiple loans?**  
A: Yes, subject to approval and salary capacity.

**Q: What if I can't pay an installment?**  
A: Contact HR immediately. Arrangements can be made.

**Q: Is there interest charged?**  
A: Check your company policy. System supports interest-free or interest-bearing loans.

**Q: Can I cancel a pending request?**  
A: Contact HR before approval to withdraw request.
