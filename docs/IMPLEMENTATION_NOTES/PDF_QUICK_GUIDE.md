# PDF Download - Quick User Guide

## 🎯 Quick Steps to Download Leave Request PDF

### For Employees

1. **Navigate to Leave Section**
   - Click on "Leave" in the main navigation

2. **View Your Leave Requests**
   - Look at the "My Leave Requests" table
   - Find requests with status "Approved" (green badge)

3. **Download PDF**
   - Click the **"📥 PDF"** button in the actions column
   - PDF will open in a new tab
   - Use your browser's download/save function

### For HR/Managers

1. **Navigate to Leave Section**
   - Click on "Leave" in the main navigation

2. **View All Leave Requests**
   - Switch to "All Requests" or "Pending Approvals" tab
   - Find requests with status "Approved"

3. **Download Any Approved PDF**
   - Click the **"📥 PDF"** button for any approved request
   - PDF opens in new tab with full details

## 📄 What's in the PDF?

### Employee Information
- Full name
- Department
- Position  
- Date employed
- Request date

### Leave Details
- Leave type (Annual, Sick, etc.)
- Reason for leave
- Start date → End date
- Total days requested
- Resumption date

### Signatures (Automatic!)
- ✅ **Employee signature** (from your profile)
- ✅ **HOD approval signature** + date
- ✅ **Accountant approval signature** + date
- ✅ **MD approval signature** + date

---

## 📸 How to Upload Your Signature

### Step 1: Go to Profile
- Click your name in top right
- Select "Profile"

### Step 2: Upload Signature
- Scroll to **"Signature"** section
- Click "Upload Signature"
- Choose your signature image (PNG recommended)
- Click "Save"

### Step 3: Done!
- Your signature will now appear on all future PDFs
- Already-approved requests will use your signature too

---

## 💡 Signature Tips

✅ **Best Format**: PNG with transparent background  
✅ **Recommended Size**: 600 x 200 pixels  
✅ **File Size**: Keep under 200KB  
✅ **Quality**: Clear and legible when scaled  
✅ **Professional**: Use actual signature or typed name

## ❓ Troubleshooting

### "PDF button not showing"
- ✅ Make sure leave request is **approved** (not pending/rejected)
- ✅ Check you're logged in with correct permissions

### "Signature not showing in PDF"
- ✅ Upload your signature in Profile section first
- ✅ Verify signature image uploaded successfully
- ✅ PDF will show name if no signature uploaded

### "Cannot download PDF"
- ✅ Check internet connection
- ✅ Try refreshing the page
- ✅ Clear browser cache and try again
